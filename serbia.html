Arrows mark parties still active, drawn to October 2026; a square end marks deregistration. Year-only dates start at the beginning of that year; end years are drawn through the end of the year. Each row is one organisation: all versions sharing an entity ID are drawn together, with a tick at each name change; affiliation changes show as a change of colour. The tag beside each abbreviation shows the current affiliation. Parties with two affiliations are striped. The database is still being completed, so grey can also mean missing data, and where a membership's start year is not recorded the colour fades in from grey instead of starting at a fixed date. Membership colour never starts before the affiliation (or its earliest predecessor) was founded; Europarty colour starts no earlier than 2004, when Regulation 2004/2003 gave European political parties formal status. Colours follow the Political Party Database family palette (Poguntke et al., 2025). Hover or focus a row for the full record.
